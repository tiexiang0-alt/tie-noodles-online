Customer policies

These policies apply to eligible purchases made directly from XIANG TIE. Marketplace purchases may also be subject to the applicable TikTok Shop or eBay terms. Nothing in these policies excludes rights that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law.

Shipping and delivery

Orders are normally dispatched within 1–7 business days after payment and order confirmation. Delivery time begins after dispatch and depends on destination and carrier. Shipping availability, charges and any estimated delivery date are disclosed before the order is confirmed. Tracking is provided where supported by the selected service.

Customers must provide an accurate delivery address. Please contact us promptly if an address needs correction. We cannot guarantee changes after dispatch.

Returns, refunds and exchanges

For change-of-mind requests, unused, unopened and resalable non-perishable goods may be returned within 14 days of delivery after contacting us for approval and return instructions. Unless the item is faulty or incorrect, the customer is responsible for return postage and original shipping charges are not refundable.

Perishable goods are not eligible for change-of-mind returns. If an item is faulty, damaged, unsafe, materially different from its description, incorrect or missing, contact us with your order details and supporting photos where appropriate. Depending on the issue, we will arrange a repair, replacement, refund or other remedy required by Australian Consumer Law. Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method.

Order cancellation

To request cancellation, contact us as soon as possible. We can cancel and refund an order before it has been dispatched. Once dispatched, the returns policy applies. XIANG TIE does not offer memberships, subscriptions or automatic recurring payments through this website.

Privacy

We collect information reasonably needed to answer enquiries, confirm orders, take payment, deliver goods, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. This may include your name, contact details, delivery address and order history. Stripe and marketplace platforms process payment information under their own privacy terms; XIANG TIE does not store complete card details.

We do not sell personal information. We may share necessary information with payment processors, delivery providers, marketplace platforms, professional advisers and authorities where required by law. You may contact us to ask about personal information we hold about you.

Terms of sale and website use

Product availability, specifications and prices may change. The applicable description and price are those shown and confirmed before payment. An order is accepted when XIANG TIE confirms it. We may cancel and refund an order if stock is unavailable, pricing is materially incorrect, delivery is not possible or fraud is suspected.

Customers must use this website lawfully and provide accurate order information. Product images may be representative where expressly stated; the relevant marketplace listing or order confirmation contains the final product details. Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under Australian Consumer Law.
